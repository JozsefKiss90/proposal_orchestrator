# Strategic positioning

**Status: approved and frozen, 30 September 2026.** Companion to `concept_note.md`. Same reading
convention: every phrase in double quotes is a verbatim slice of the Tier 2B call extract, and partners
are pseudonyms. The approval record is
`docs/tier4_orchestration_state/decision_log/demo-concept_2026-09-30.json`.

## The competitive picture

The topic carries EUR 10,000,000 and funds two projects at around EUR 5,000,000 each. A single-stage
deadline means one submission and no resubmission inside the call. Two slots against a topic this broad
means the panel will see proposals that cover the scope and differ in how they bind it together.

Breadth alone does not differentiate here. Every serious proposal will name three realms, omics, FAIR
workflows and AI mapping, because the scope lists them. The differentiator is whether the pieces are
one system or a portfolio.

## Where this project differs

One pressure gradient carries all three ecosystem realms. Most consortia will assemble three
demonstration cases from three unrelated sites, because that is how partner capability distributes. This
project runs farmland, the freshwater ecotones receiving its runoff and the coastal waters downstream as
one causal chain.

That choice has an evaluable consequence. A harmonisation decision taken in farmland is tested twice
more, in realms that did not design it. The scope asks for integration "across terrestrial, freshwater
and marine ecosystems", and a shared gradient is evidence of integration rather than an assertion of it.

The second difference is the validation protocol. `P01` brings leakage-safe predictive validation and
statistical experimental design. The third expected outcome asks for in-situ data usable as "training
and validation resources", and usable means a split a downstream modeller can trust. A field archive
released without a declared split protocol looks like a dataset and behaves like a trap.

The third difference is the route to agriculture. `P05` runs a multi-country user network of
agricultural holdings. The scope names agriculture as a sector that biodiversity data should reach, and
an existing channel is worth more to that claim than a planned one.

## Fit to the evaluation criteria

Tier 2A records three award criteria for an RIA: Excellence, Impact, and Quality and efficiency of the
implementation.

| Criterion | What this concept offers | What is still missing |
|---|---|---|
| Excellence | One chain from molecules to satellites, tested on a shared gradient, with a declared validation protocol | No methodology vault and no cited literature. Phase 2 has nothing to lift a state-of-the-art claim from |
| Impact | Outputs shaped to four named reporting instruments, crosswalks to the European habitat classification, and an existing uptake channel into farming | Key performance indicators are not set. Phase 5 builds them from the seeds, which are not authored yet |
| Implementation | Twelve partners with non-overlapping roles, one lead per layer and one lead per demonstration case | No work packages, no timeline and no budget. The topic is lump sum, so Phase 8 stays blocked until an external planner response arrives |

## Why now

The scope rests on an argument about timing. In-situ collections were built before satellite integration
was a design goal, and the topic states the consequence: they "lack the structure, interface and
metadata required to support advanced analytics, including AI". Retrofitting them is cheaper than
recollecting, and it is only worth doing once the typologies and metadata standards are stable enough to
retrofit against.

The project sits on that point. It reworks an existing multi-season archive rather than starting a new
collection, and it publishes the reworking as protocol rather than as a dataset.

## What weakens the position

Three weaknesses, stated for the operator rather than argued away.

1. **Half the consortium is invented.** `P07` to `P12` are fictional gap partners. For the demo this is
   the point, because it exercises the uncertainty ledger. For the proposal it means the composition
   condition and four cross-cutting duties rest on Assumed declarations.
2. **The geography is thin.** Ten of twelve partners sit in three country slots and five sit in `C1`. A
   topic asking for use cases in "Member States and Associated Countries" invites a panel comment on
   geographic balance.
3. **The concept has no evidence base yet.** The source materials ticket is blocked on this approval.
   Until it runs, every mechanism here is design intent at Assumed, and no claim carries a citation.

## What this positioning is not

It is not a draft of the Excellence section, and it does not anticipate one. Phase 8 is hard-blocked for
this topic until a validated lump-sum response exists, and `CLAUDE.md` §13.4 covers preparatory drafting
as well as drafting. This note stays at the level the operator needs to approve or reject a direction.
