Design and run the validation protocol on Hungarian tomato farms accessed via the associated partner AgroVIR: collect non-destructive physiological ground truth (RQ4: chlorophyll fluorescence, stomatal conductance, canopy/leaf temperature, spectral reflectance), assess uncertainty calibration (RQ5: 90% prediction intervals contain the truth about 90% of the time), define pilot success as the RQ5 target met on the validation set (RQ7), and test Route B's patch-covariation assumption via a co-located seasonal campaign measuring the target crop and the proxy patch simultaneously (RQ9), including the control-stand variant.
