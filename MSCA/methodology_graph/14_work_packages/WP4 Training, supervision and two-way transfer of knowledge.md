MSCA-PF structural work package for the researcher's training (scientific, management, transferable skills), supervision, and two-way transfer of knowledge between researcher and host. Content: a Career Development Plan prepared jointly by supervisor and fellow and submitted as a project deliverable at the start of the action, then reviewed and updated during the fellowship; structured transferable-skills training covering the MSCA-PF mandatory dimensions - digital and AI skills (including responsible use of generative AI in remote-sensing and crop-water modelling and scientific coding), knowledge valorisation, innovation and entrepreneurship, research integrity, and open science / FAIR data stewardship; supervision by Dr. András Jung through regular research reviews in the host's geoinformatics and remote-sensing group; and two-way transfer of knowledge - the fellow contributes plant-physiology and non-destructive stress-phenotyping expertise (biological ground truth), and takes up the host's geoinformatics, remote-sensing and hyperspectral/multisensor-fusion methods and infrastructure. Specific quantitative training/career targets are not asserted (none operator-provided) and are to be set in the Career Development Plan.
