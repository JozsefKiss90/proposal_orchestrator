Enhance the researcher's scientific, technical and transferable competences and career perspectives through the fellowship's training, supervision and two-way transfer of knowledge (MSCA-PF Impact criterion 2.1 / Excellence criterion 1.3). The two-way transfer is bidirectional: the fellow brings plant-physiology and non-destructive stress-phenotyping expertise (biological ground truth) to the host's geoinformatics and remote-sensing group; the host provides geoinformatics, remote sensing, and hyperspectral/multisensor-fusion methods and infrastructure to the fellow.
