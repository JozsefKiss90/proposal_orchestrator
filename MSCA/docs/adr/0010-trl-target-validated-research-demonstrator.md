# TRL target: validated research demonstrator (TRL 5)

**Status:** accepted (2026-06-30)

**Context.** RQ10. The sources present the MVP as a *standalone research demonstrator*, with AgroVIR-like partners assisting (feedback / farmer-access / testing / validation) rather than owning or operating it, and are explicit: "do not promise an operational product." Every earlier scoping decision caps the honest maturity claim.

**Decision.** Target **TRL 5 — technology validated in a relevant environment** — on a ≈3→5 trajectory. The deliverable is a **validated research demonstrator** run end-to-end on real pilot fields. The integrated field demonstrator may *reach toward* TRL 6 ("demonstrated in relevant environment"), but the headline claim is **TRL 5**, and it is **explicitly not an operational pilot (TRL 7)**.

- **MVP = standalone research demonstrator** surfacing the diagnosis, the simulated options, and the recommendation *with its uncertainty*. **Partners (AgroVIR-like) assist**, they do not own or operate it.
- The **light user-acceptance endpoint** (ADR-0009) is the TRL-readiness signal — appropriately light for TRL 5, not full product UX.

**Why.** RQ7 delivers *efficacy in validated hindcast + feasibility in the field* — precisely "validated in a relevant environment" (TRL 5). RQ9's within-sample-only transferability and RQ7's deferral of a population-level field-profit claim forbid an operational (TRL 7) claim, and the sources warn against promising a product.

## Considered options

- **TRL 4 (validated in lab)** — too conservative: the chain runs end-to-end on real fields with real users, beyond lab validation.
- **TRL 6–7 (operational pilot)** — rejected: contradicts the within-sample transferability and simulation-based efficacy scope, and the sources' "no operational product."

## Consequences

- Work-plan and impact claims are capped at demonstrator/validation; partner roles stay assisting.
- **Future work (explicitly deferred):** operational pilot (TRL 6–7), population-level transferability, multi-crop generalisation, and productization — named as a credible follow-on trajectory rather than a limitation.
