The central research gap is the lack of physiologically validated crop water-stress models that are tested for temporal and spatial transferability across sensing scales and then prospectively evaluated under independent commercial-field conditions before operational deployment.

FIELDWISE closes this gap by developing a physiologically anchored model from a five-season tomato dataset, validating it on unseen years, freezing it before the new field season, testing it prospectively in an independent commercial field, and translating only validated functions into the DrR decision-support MVP.
