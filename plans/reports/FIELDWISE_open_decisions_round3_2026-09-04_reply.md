A-2 
FIELDWISE will generate three citable scientific outputs: (1) a physiologically grounded processing-tomato water-stress prediction model with documented uncertainty and unseen-year performance; (2) a prospectively quantified research-to-commercial-field transfer gap, showing how much predictive performance survives temporal, environmental and sensing-scale transfer; and (3) a crop-transfer specification distinguishing the potentially reusable elements of the physiology-to-prediction framework from crop-specific biological and calibration components that require recalibration and revalidation. The principal new knowledge is therefore not simply model accuracy, but quantitative evidence of whether and how a biologically grounded water-stress model remains valid when transferred from experimental data to operational commercial-field conditions.
