# Eligibility query — MSCA-PF 2026 resubmission bar

**Prepared:** 2026-08-24 · **Status:** ready to send after the one check in §1

---

## 0. Why this exists

The MSCA-PF 2026 call carries a resubmission bar at 80%. On the facts in our own records, FIELDWISE
appears to meet every condition of it. If that reading is right, the 2026 submission cannot be made
and all remaining work on it — the concept-update fold, the P2–P8 re-run, the partner confirmations
— is moot. This query settles that before any of it is spent.

**Send it today.** Sixteen days remain to the deadline; an NCP answer typically takes one to three
working days.

> **Subject:** MSCA-PF 2026 — application of the 80% resubmission restriction to a substantively new project

>
> > "proposals involving the same recruiting organisation (and for Global Postdoctoral Fellowships also
> > the same associated partner hosting the outgoing phase) and individual researcher submitted for the
> > MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2025 call and having received a score of less than 80% must not be
> > resubmitted."

> Our situation is as follows.
>
> A proposal was submitted to the MSCA-PF 2025 call: **PLANTDIGISENSE**, proposal number **101284764**,

> different partner configuration. It is not a re-serialisation of the 2025 text.
>
> My questions:
>
> 1. **Does the restriction apply to us?** That is, does "must not be resubmitted" key on the identity
>    of the recruiting organisation and researcher regardless of the project's scientific content, or
>    does a substantively new project by the same pair fall outside it? This is the question that
>    determines whether we submit at all.
>
> 2. **If it applies — is the condition conjunctive?** The wording pairs "same recruiting organisation"
>    *and* "individual researcher". We are considering moving the recruiting organisation to a
>    different Hungarian legal entity — **HUN-REN Centre for Agricultural Research (ATK),
>    Martonvásár** — with the same researcher, and with ELTE retained as an associated partner.

>
>    a. Is "recruiting organisation" read at the level of the beneficiary legal entity / PIC?
>
>    b. Would ELTE's continued involvement as an **associated partner** (rather than beneficiary)
>       re-trigger the restriction?

>       that they may obtain their own PICs. Is there anything about a **recently registered PIC**

>    the researcher or the host beyond rejection of that proposal?

> 4. **Does the bar recur?** Does a score below 80% in the 2026 call similarly bar the same

>
> Given the 9 September deadline I would be grateful for any indication you can give quickly, even
> provisionally.
>
> With thanks,
> [name, role, institution, contact]

## 4. Why question 4 matters more than it looks

It is the question that decides the strategy even if the answer to question 1 is favourable.

If a sub-80% score bars the following call, then submitting a proposal that has been rebuilt in
sixteen days — after the host-side data owner has questioned its methodology, with the satellite
strand unevidenced, the UAV band set unknown, MVCRI instrument comparability unconfirmed and the
MATE commercial-IP position unresolved — is not a free attempt. A weak submission would cost the
2027 attempt as well.

That inverts the usual instinct. "Submit something to keep the option alive" is exactly backwards
under this rule: submitting weak is what closes the option.

## Appendix — the evidence behind §0

**The rule.** Confirmed in two independent sources with identical wording: the REA MSCA-PF 2026
presentation and the MSCA-PF 2026 Guide for Applicants. Award criteria for the call are
Excellence 50% / Impact 30% / Implementation 20%; Seal of Excellence at ≥85%.

**The facts.** From `plans/fieldwise_operator_input_pack.md`, section "The previous proposal —
PLANTDIGISENSE" and the CV block above it:

| Rule condition | FIELDWISE | Verified? |
|---|---|:--:|
| Submitted to MSCA-PF 2025 | PLANTDIGISENSE, HORIZON-MSCA-2025-PF, proposal 101284764 | From CV — check |
| Same recruiting organisation | ELTE — host of both | Yes, in `selected_call.json` |
| Same individual researcher | Dr. Rositsa Cholakova — fellow in both | Yes |
| Score < 80% | 70.40%, against a 70.00 threshold | **From CV — verify (§1)** |

**The ambiguity.** The rule's text keys on organisation and researcher, not on project content. The
mechanical reading is the more likely one, because it is what an automated eligibility check can
verify — but it is not stated either way in the sources found, which is precisely why this goes to
the NCP rather than being decided internally.

**What our own records did and did not do.** The input pack recorded the 70.40% score and reasoned
about it strategically — "FIELDWISE is therefore a resubmission that needs to move a score, not
repair a failure" — and the draft answers the prior ESR's criticisms in three separate places
(`strategic_positioning.md`). So the previous submission was known throughout. It appears simply
never to have been checked against the resubmission bar.
