## Short answer

**The supervisor swap is right on the science, and I'd argue for it independently of eligibility.**
**The host switch probably does clear the resubmission bar.**
**Neither saves the 2026 submission**, because there is a second eligibility problem the switch does not touch, and because it destroys the part of the draft the concept update had left standing.

For a 2027 submission, this is a strong configuration and worth building deliberately.

---

## 1. Does it clear the resubmission bar?

Probably yes. The rule's condition is conjunctive:

> "proposals involving the same recruiting organisation … **and** individual researcher … having received a score of less than 80% must not be resubmitted."

Break either limb and the bar should not bite. Changing the recruiting organisation breaks the first. ELTE and a HUN-REN institute are different legal entities with different PICs.

**Do not treat this as settled.** It is question 2 in the NCP query, now sharpened to name the specific institute. Two things could complicate it: whether REA reads "recruiting organisation" at the level of the legal entity or looks through to substantively the same project and team, and whether ELTE remaining involved as an associated partner (which the plan needs — see §5) is enough to re-trigger it.

## 3. Why the supervisor swap is scientifically right, not a workaround

This is the strongest part of the idea, and it deserves to be argued on its merits rather than defended as an eligibility manoeuvre.

The concept update moves the project's centre of gravity. With the Sentinel-2 strand unevidenced (Δ1) and the dataset revealed as small, nested and manual (Δ3), FIELDWISE stops being a remote-sensing scaling project and becomes a **process-based, physiologically-grounded, small-sample crop modelling project**. Hierarchical and Bayesian methods move from fourth in the WP3 list to the front.

That is precisely Hollós's field. And Tier 3 already records the direction of travel: the co-supervisor's person-months were raised from 1.5 to 2.0 in August "when Dr. Hollós was confirmed responsible for model development and testing", with his sessions moved from fortnightly to weekly through WP3–WP4. The draft's §1.2 already calls him "the methodological lead and quality assurance for model development and testing." **The formal roles are already out of step with the actual division of labour, and the concept update widens the gap.**

There's a second fit. The fellow is a plant physiologist working on abiotic stress in *Solanum lycopersicum*. ATK has a Department of Plant Physiology and Metabolomics and a stress-resistance programme. ELTE's Institute of Cartography and Geoinformatics does not. If the satellite strand goes, the cartography institute is no longer the obvious home for this work — an agricultural research institute is.

**Cost to weigh:** Jung is a full Professor with the remote-sensing publication record that carries §1.3's "qualifications and track record of the supervisors". Hollós is a Researcher/PhD. Supervisor standing is scored. Keeping Jung as co-supervisor retains much of it, but the headline supervisor's CV gets thinner, and that is a real trade against a criterion worth 50% of the score.

## 4. Why it does not save 2026 — the mobility rule

**This is the decisive point, and the host switch does nothing about it.**

The MSCA mobility rule is about the **beneficiary's country**, not the beneficiary's institution. HUN-REN ATK is in Hungary. ELTE is in Hungary. Switching between them changes the mobility arithmetic by exactly zero.

And that arithmetic is already failing. From the verified analysis in project memory, window 2023-09-09 → 2026-09-09:

| Period | Months in Hungary |
|---|---:|
| Career break, Budapest, 2023-09-09 → 2024-05-31 | 8.76 |
| KPMG Hungary, 2024-06-01 → 2024-10-01 | 4.03 |
| Erasmus at ELTE, 2026 | 3.02 |
| **Total** | **15.81** vs a **12-month cap** |

The first two are contiguous — one unbroken residence whose 12-month allowance expired 2024-09-09 while she stayed to 2024-10-01. Over the line before the Erasmus counts at all.

The current position rests on an operator override of 2026-08-11 on the basis that the CV will be rectified. Memory flags that as load-bearing and fragile: KPMG is an employer of record and the Erasmus is documented by ELTE. One small consolation if the host changes — the Erasmus is no longer documented by the beneficiary itself — but the three months still count toward Hungary.

**So a 2026 submission carries two independent eligibility problems. The host switch addresses one of them.**

## 6. Four further problems, in order of severity

**a) The host would no longer own the key instrument.** §3.2's entire host-capacity case is ELTE's GIS and Remote Sensing Laboratory — the DJI Matrice 350 RTK, the Zenmuse P1/L1/H20T, the Ultris S5 and Cubert CUVIS hyperspectral cameras. Under Δ1 the **drone becomes the primary spatial instrument of the whole project**. If ATK is the beneficiary, the project's central instrument sits at an associated partner. Whether ATK has its own UAV and hyperspectral capability is not documented in what I could reach — **check this first**; it determines whether §3.2 can be written at all.

**b) HUN-REN ATK would apply on a new PIC.** HUN-REN was legally transformed in October 2025, and the Commission's Central Validation Service only confirmed in **February 2026** that the new institutes could obtain PICs as independent legal entities. Two consequences: the PIC must be registered and validated before submission — check ATK's status urgently if you pursue this — and a legal entity under a year old is a *weaker* hosting-capacity story than an established university, not a stronger one. The criterion asks about capacity and hosting arrangements.

**c) EI-06 probably breaks.** The expected impact "Enhanced integration of research results into higher education and teaching at host organisations" is currently met by co-designing an ELTE MSc module unit. HUN-REN is a research network, not a university. ATK's teaching route would be doctoral-school affiliation at best. This is gate-relevant: `phase_05_gate` predicate g06_p04 requires every Tier 2B expected impact to carry a mapped project output, and EI-06 was already the thinnest of the six after the MSc-thesis element was withdrawn on 2026-08-14.

**d) The host relationship no longer predates the project.** The input pack noted this as a quiet strength of ELTE: the host and supervisor arrangement existed before FIELDWISE, evidenced by the 2026 Erasmus internship and the 2025 submission. The fellow's recorded Hungarian links are to MATE and ELTE — **none to HUN-REN**. A host relationship assembled in sixteen days reads differently from one with history.

## 9. Revision — under the operator's rulings of 2026-08-24

**a) Does the switch clear the resubmission bar? Probably yes, under either reading of the rule.**

The bar's conditions are conjunctive — same recruiting organisation *and* same researcher. Two
readings are possible and **both are cleared**:

- *Mechanical* (the pair identifies the proposal): changing the beneficiary from ELTE to
  HUN-REN ATK breaks the first limb. Bar does not apply.
- *Substantive* ("resubmitted" implies the same proposal): FIELDWISE is a different project from
  PLANTDIGISENSE — different acronym, concept, RQs, hypotheses, work plan and partners. Bar would
  not have applied even with ELTE.

A textual point that strengthens the position: the rule *does* reach an associated partner, but only
"for Global Postdoctoral Fellowships … the associated partner hosting the outgoing phase." The
drafters knew how to extend it beyond the beneficiary and chose to do so in one narrow case. For a
European Fellowship, **ELTE continuing as an ordinary associated partner should not re-trigger it.**
Still worth putting to the NCP, since it is the one place the arrangement could be attacked.

**b) Mobility is orthogonal, not solved.** The switch changes nothing either way — ATK is in Hungary,
as ELTE is. If the corrected residence data holds, the proposal passes under either host; if it does
not, neither host saves it. Note that the corrected figure is exactly the passing scenario already
identified in [[fieldwise-researcher]]: removing the 8.76-month career break leaves 4.03 (KPMG) +
3.02 (Erasmus) = **7.05 months against a 12-month cap**, ~5 months of margin. The remaining
requirement is documentary, not arithmetic — the CV sits inside Part B-2 §4 and its dates must agree
with the Part A declaration.

**c) PIC — my earlier warning was overstated.** The Funding & Tenders Portal states that a newly
created PIC is "provisional (declared/non-valid status) until it is formally validated at a later
stage", and that "if your proposal is successful, you will be contacted for the validation of your
PIC registration data." So ATK needs *a registered* PIC, not a *validated* one, to submit. This is
a task, not a blocker. Confirm with the NCP anyway, since HUN-REN's post-transformation registrations
are recent.

**d) The change that matters: the supervisor swap loses its scientific rationale.**

§3 argued the swap was right because the concept update moved the project's centre of gravity from
remote sensing to process-based modelling. **With the methodology unchanged and the satellite strand
primary, that argument no longer holds** — and the position inverts:

- O2, the hyperspectral-to-Sentinel transfer, remains a headline objective.
- §1.2 assigns the sensor-domain transfer methods and the satellite-scaling design (H2) to the host's
  expertise, in the person of Prof. Jung — remote sensing, hyperspectral imaging, field spectroscopy.
- `capabilities.json` records that ELTE's Ultris S5 and Cubert CUVIS "are the institute's own
  hyperspectral capability, which is what the §1.2 hyperspectral-to-Sentinel resampling needs."

Under the switch, the beneficiary would supply **neither the central expertise nor the central
instruments** for the project's headline methodological claim — both sit at an associated partner —
and the lead supervisor would be the process-modelling specialist rather than the remote-sensing one.
That is a coherence problem across Excellence 1.3 and Implementation 3.2: precisely the two sections
the switch already forces to be rewritten from scratch.

It is not fatal, and it is answerable — ELTE as associated partner with a defined secondment, Jung as
named co-supervisor, ATK's own capability set out honestly. But it must be *written*, and it is
sixteen days. The question worth asking before committing: **if the science stays satellite-primary,
is ATK the right host for it, or is it the host that happens to have Hollós?**
